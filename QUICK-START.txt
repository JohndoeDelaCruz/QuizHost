QUIZHOST 0.1.4 BETA 3 — CLASSROOM PILOT

1. Open release\QuizHost-0.1.4-beta.3-Windows.exe on the teacher's Windows x64 laptop.
2. Click Host game on the sample quiz.
3. Choose a play mode and open the room.

UPDATING FROM AN EARLIER BUILD
Finish the current game and close the earlier QuizHost window.
Run the new EXE. Saved quizzes and finished results use the same data folder.

INDIVIDUAL PHONE MODE
Connect the laptop and phones to the same local Wi-Fi.
Open the address displayed in Setup & connection on each phone.
Students enter the game PIN and their name.
Click Start game, reveal answers, and continue to the final scoreboard.
Every question starts with a 3-2-1 countdown, then the full answer timer.

SHARED-SCREEN TEAM MODE
Choose Better together. Enter one team name per line (2–8 teams).
Start, choose a tile, let the active team answer aloud, then reveal.
Mark Correct or Missed it. QuizHost rotates the teams for you.
Student phones and Wi-Fi are not needed for this mode.

ANIMATIONS
Answer tiles, feedback, leaderboard changes, and the final podium animate.
Workspace pages and quiz-editor question tabs have short entrance transitions.
Click Animations on in the header to use reduced motion on that device.
The app also follows the device's reduced-motion preference.

AFTER CLASS
Finished scores are saved under Results. Export CSV to keep a copy.
Use Back up in the library to export your quizzes.

BEFORE FINAL DELIVERY
This is an unsigned beta build. Run a pilot on the school's laptop.
Test the intended Wi-Fi with real phones before promising 50 players.
The current room ends if the teacher app closes. Finished results and
saved quizzes remain on disk. Keep the laptop awake during a game.

See README.md in the source archive for development and network details.
