QUIZHOST 0.1.4 BETA 5 — CLASSROOM PILOT

1. Open release\QuizHost-0.1.4-beta.5-Windows.exe on the teacher's Windows x64 laptop.
2. Click Host game on the sample quiz.
3. Choose a play mode and open the room.

UPDATING FROM AN EARLIER BUILD
Finish the current game and close the earlier QuizHost window.
Run the new EXE. Saved quizzes and finished results use the same data folder.

INDIVIDUAL PHONE MODE
Connect the laptop and phones to the same local Wi-Fi.
Open the address displayed in Setup & connection on each phone.
Students enter the game PIN and their name.
Click Start game. Students receive random avatars that stay on reconnection.
Each question and optional photo appear with a five-second reading bar.
Answer choices then open with the full answer timer. Answers reveal when
everyone has answered, time expires, or the teacher clicks Reveal answer.
After feedback, watch the animated rankings and continue to the next question.

SHARED-SCREEN TEAM MODE
Choose Better together. Enter one team name per line (2–8 teams).
Start, choose a tile, let the active team answer aloud, then reveal.
Mark Correct or Missed it. Watch the rankings, then continue to rotate teams.
Student phones and Wi-Fi are not needed for this mode.

ANIMATIONS
Answer tiles, feedback, leaderboard changes, and the final podium animate.
Workspace pages and quiz-editor question tabs have short entrance transitions.
Click Animations on in the header to use reduced motion on that device.
The app also follows the device's reduced-motion preference.

OFFLINE MUSIC
Teacher music changes with the lobby, reading, answering, rankings, and podium.
Students hear their own answer-result sounds. Student background music is
off by default and can be enabled in Sound settings beside the speaker button.
Each device can mute sound and adjust volume, Background music, and Game sounds.
These settings are saved on that device. Open/join a game or tap Enable sound
to activate playback. After reloading or a device interruption, tap it again
if needed. All music files are included; internet access is not required.

QUESTION PHOTOS
In the quiz editor, click Add image below the question text.
Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP file up to 10 MB. Add an optional description.
Replace or remove the image as needed, then save the quiz.
The photo appears with the question in both play modes. Click Enlarge
and use the zoom controls to read details. Photos work offline and are
included in quiz backups. Photo backups require Beta 4 or later.

AFTER CLASS
Finished scores are saved under Results. Export CSV to keep a copy.
Use Back up in the library to export your quizzes.
When moving all data to another laptop, copy the entire data folder,
including quizhost.sqlite and the images folder, with the app closed.

BEFORE FINAL DELIVERY
This is an unsigned beta build. Run a pilot on the school's laptop.
Test the intended Wi-Fi with real phones before promising 50 players.
The current room ends if the teacher app closes. Finished results and
saved quizzes remain on disk. Keep the laptop awake during a game.

See README.md in the source archive for development and network details.
